# Chapter 6 coverage map (reference ch6-7 -> Quantica ch6). Coverage checklist ONLY.

6.1 Ratios, Simple and Subtle   <- ref 6.1, 6.2
    common multiplier k; part-to-part vs part-to-whole; 3-part ratios; a ratio statement is
    a linear equation; ratios that change; chaining x:y with y:z; a ratio pins proportion only.

6.2 Conversion Factors          <- ref 6.3
    cancel units by writing the factor as a fraction; chains of factors; converting RATES
    (unlike units, e.g. per-hour to per-second); ref's stressed idea "ratios build equations
    even in problems that do not look like ratio problems".

6.3 Percents                    <- ref 6.4
    percent means per hundred; percent <-> fraction <-> decimal both ways; a% of b; finding the
    whole from a part and its percent; common conversions worth knowing by sight.

6.4 Percent Problems            <- ref 6.5
    x increased by k% is x(1 + k/100), decreased is x(1 - k/100); SUCCESSIVE percent changes
    MULTIPLY and do not add (up 20% then down 20% does not return); percent of a percent;
    when every fact is relative, assign the variable yourself; mixture by amount of substance.
    NOTE: 5.5 deliberately kept percent-mixture out, so this is its first appearance.

6.5 Direct and Inverse Proportion  <- ref 7.1, 7.2
    directly proportional means x/y is constant; inversely proportional means xy is constant;
    find k from one pair then use it; telling the two apart from a description or a table.

6.6 Joint Proportion and Rates  <- ref 7.3, 7.4
    x jointly proportional to several variables, x = k*y*z/w; group the varying quantities on
    one side and the constants on the other; d = rt; work problems via amount of work done;
    two objects moving (toward, apart, catching up).
